Learn About Enzymes & pH

Understanding the relationship between pH and enzyme functionality

What are Enzymes?

Enzymes are biological catalysts that speed up chemical reactions in living organisms. They are proteins with specific three-dimensional structures that allow them to bind to specific molecules called substrates.

  • Made of amino acid chains folded into specific shapes
  • Have active sites that bind to substrates
  • Lower activation energy for reactions
  • Are not consumed in the reaction

pH and Its Effects

pH measures how acidic or basic a solution is. Enzymes are sensitive to pH changes because their structure depends on the ionization state of amino acid residues.

  • pH 0-6: Acidic (more H+ ions)
  • pH 7: Neutral (pure water)
  • pH 8-14: Basic (more OH- ions)
  • Each enzyme has an optimal pH range

Enzyme Denaturation

Extreme pH values can cause irreversible changes to enzyme structure, leading to loss of function. This process is called denaturation.

  • Changes in protein folding pattern
  • Loss of active site shape
  • Irreversible in most cases
  • Results in complete loss of activity

Pepsin - Stomach Enzyme

Pepsin works in the highly acidic environment of the stomach, breaking down proteins into smaller peptides.

Optimal pH: 1.5 - 2.5
Location: Stomach
Function: Protein digestion

Amylase - Starch Breakdown

Amylase breaks down starch into simple sugars and works optimally at neutral pH, found in saliva and pancreatic juice.

Optimal pH: 6.5 - 7.5
Location: Saliva, Pancreas
Function: Starch digestion

Trypsin - Protein Cutter

Trypsin is a pancreatic enzyme that cleaves proteins at specific amino acid sequences, working optimally in basic conditions.

Optimal pH: 7.5 - 8.5
Location: Pancreas
Function: Protein cleavage

About This Project

A comprehensive tool for understanding enzyme behavior

Project Overview

The pH and Enzyme Activity Predictor is an educational tool designed to help students and researchers understand how pH affects enzyme activity. It provides both theoretical knowledge and practical prediction capabilities.

Features:

  • Interactive pH slider and enzyme selector
  • Real-time activity calculations
  • Visual pH scale with enzyme ranges
  • Activity vs pH charts for each enzyme
  • Educational content about enzyme biochemistry
  • Responsive design for all devices

Technical Details

This application uses mathematical models to simulate enzyme behavior based on established biochemical principles.

Calculation Method:

  • Bell curve distribution for optimal activity
  • Exponential decay outside optimal ranges
  • Complete activity loss at denaturation thresholds
  • Real-time chart generation using Chart.js

Technologies Used:

HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript ES6+ Chart.js Python 3.x
